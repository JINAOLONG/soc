## 5. Review D01–D10

For every decision, provide:

1. Exact current proposal and version/hash.
2. Source-supported requirements versus new proposed assumptions.
3. Internal consistency checks and their actual evidence.
4. Scientific interpretation risks.
5. Missing definitions or ambiguous edge cases.
6. A recommendation: ACCEPT AS PROPOSED, REVISE BEFORE APPROVAL, or REJECT.
7. Exact suggested changes, where necessary.
8. Whether human approval is still required.

### D01–D04: Workload construction

Verify all six DAG templates, node/edge counts, acyclicity, node identity, role inheritance, size transformation, compatibility sets, positive execution costs, edge data sizes, all nine directed communication-type pairs, units, keyed perturbation identity, arrival windows, deadlines and control transformations.

Check that no hidden nodes, implicit edges or undeclared random inputs exist.

Verify that all proposed numeric values are explicitly labelled synthetic and are not attributed to literature or hardware measurements.

### D05: Trial matrix

Independently recalculate:

- 36 configurations.
- 10 registered calibration seeds.
- 2,520 planned trial slots.
- 1,800 MAIN slots.
- 360 H_ISOLATED slots.
- 360 LOW_COMPETITION slots.
- 1,080 planned workload inputs.
- At most 5,040 engine invocations including one registered replay per trial.

Check exact seed-to-configuration-to-condition-to-method mappings, uniqueness, execution order, budget fields and selection rules.

Verify that both A/B configurations remain fully accounted for even when only one is selected for final condition-level analysis.

Engineering timing is outside this proposal and must remain explicitly unfrozen.

### D06: Feasibility and load classification

Adversarially examine:

- Whether the H_ISOLATED control can establish only a control-specific feasibility witness.
- Whether the LOW_COMPETITION control is interpreted without claiming it is identical to the main workload.
- Whether the optimistic path lower bound is mathematically valid and implemented without scheduler access to hidden truth.
- Whether load bins are mutually exclusive and exhaustive.
- Whether all miss-rate and pressure statistics use the declared denominator and seed aggregation.
- Whether A-before-B selection is deterministic and independent of candidate advantage and mapping-opportunity outcomes.
- Whether invalid, failed, incomplete, naturally infeasible and valid-but-off-target results remain distinct.

Identify any edge case that could permit an invalid or infeasible configuration to be selected.

### D07: Mapping mechanism

Verify that M1 uses only the frozen public observation boundary, fixed task order, declared ready-set truncation and registered resource constraints.

Check that the baseline is included in the enumerated feasible set, that the local objective and L constraints are unambiguous, and that no future arrivals, unready tasks or private execution truth enter the mechanism diagnostic.

Check denominator handling, zero eligible views, repeated views, deterministic tie-breaking and independent recomputation requirements.

Verify that M2 remains descriptive and is not reported as an independent causal mapping test.

### D08: Three-state criterion

Verify the exact FINE and COARSE definitions, seed-level denominators, condition coverage, paired-seed bootstrap indexing, pointwise confidence-interval interpretation and handling of missing/invalid/incomplete inputs.

Check whether SUPPORTED, NOT_SUPPORTED and INCONCLUSIVE are logically distinct and exhaustively assigned under all valid and invalid input cases.

Do not select FINE or COARSE on the basis of observed scientific outcomes. Recommend a choice only by reference to the predeclared research objective and the limitations of each operational definition.

### D09: Runner and recovery

Check the proposed execution state machine, identity locking, raw-output preservation, actual process exit recording, replay, atomic COMPLETE publication, exact-match reuse and no-automatic-retry policy.

Separate existing contractual requirements from newly proposed clean-worktree requirements.

Identify every condition that must cause the plan to stop.

### D10: Scientific schema and lineage

Check namespace/versioning, canonicalization, workload/run/attempt IDs, actual seed and partition identity, source/config/input hashes, generator lineage, provenance references, trace validation, independent metric derivation, deterministic replay and COMPLETE criteria.

Ensure scientific artifacts cannot be created merely by relabelling P1 fixtures.

Specify the required P1 compatibility and regression tests for a future separately authorized implementation.
