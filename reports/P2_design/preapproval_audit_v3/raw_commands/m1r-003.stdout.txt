49: ## D04 — typed keyed生成、身份和控制
50: 
51: 精确对象见`P2_typed_identity_schema_v2.json`的closed `$defs`，字段不得增删或省略为默认。
52: 根schema_version=2，generator_version=`soc-p2-synthetic-v2-PROPOSED`，key domain=`soc.p2.gen.v2`。
53: 不再有含义不明的version字段。`P2_generation_spec_v2.json#/key_bindings`以LITERAL/COPY逐字段给出三种key，context路径与类型固定；gloss不是运行实例。
54: 
55: 每种key都保留actual seed十进制字符串、scenario、condition、config、整数slot、schema/gen版本。
56: arrival_jitter仅将group/node/resource/edge置空；估计因子保留group/node，resource/edge为空；
57: 实际因子再保留resource。counter固定0。方法、run/attempt、控制到达、host、wall time、调用次数
58: 不入扰动key。边和deadline没有随机draw，禁用未登记随机源。
59: 
60: SHA256规范key字节的前8字节大端q，U=q/2^64为精确有理数；因子.9+U/5，jitter=U/10。
61: 所有计算先精确有理数，每个最终数值只一次RN64 ties-to-even；私有exact estimate/deadline
62: sidecar只供生成/控制核验，不进入公共视图。v2域/键绑定改变未来draw身份；没有v1 realization
63: 可比较，也不声称v1/v2将产生同一样本。数值分布本身未变。
64: 
65: MAIN仍50slot，每slot一个H和L，共100服务；5slot块内压缩1/4；W=50P；全量排空，无warmup剔除。
66: H relative deadline=2.5*C_est、L=4*C_est，C_est从转换前E_exact取min-compatible路径，忽略通信。
67: 它不证明可行。绝对deadline按原模型RN64(arrival+relative_deadline)计算。
68: 
69: 服务ID：`{scenario}/{load}/{size}/{config_id}/s{slot:03d}/{H|L}`。
70: 节点ID：`{service_id}/{preserved_template_node_id}`。condition=`{scenario}-{load}-{size}`。
71: 
72: 控制始终从**已解析MAIN父输入**派生，保留所有被保留节点的估计/真实代价、相对deadline及
73: 边的binary64位，不重新draw。G对父MAIN全部100服务计算，两个控制使用同一个G；精确公式
74: 和一次ceil+1ms规则在generation JSON。H-only按原slot保留50H，LOW按slot、H在L前保留100服务。
75: 都改为k*G到达，故与MAIN有不同descriptor/content identity，绝不复用MAIN内容hash。
76: 先物化/解析MAIN，再派生控制；这不执行MAIN方法，也不改变D05的引擎执行顺序。
77: 
78: canonical语义对象只接受ASCII字符串、精确整数、bool/null、array/object；不接受浮点。
79: Rational为最简`{numerator:十进制字符串,denominator:正十进制字符串}`，0只用0/1，禁-0。
80: 表中的decimal词法先精确Decimal转分数，不经binary64。键ASCII排序、紧凑JSON、UTF-8、单LF，
81: 拒绝重复键和非ASCII身份。raw SHA计算实际文件原字节；语义SHA另算。运行payload单独使用
82: Python3.12 binary64 JSON规范，不能与语义参数hash混称。golden fixtures不是生产输入。

100: ## D06 — 总分类、选择与可行性
101: 
102: `P2_stage_state_contract_v2.json#/load_classification`为精确谓词来源：
103: REGISTERED→完整运行集→输入/trace/metric/replay有效→控制资格→本身declared target-load匹配→
104: ELIGIBLE或INELIGIBLE→先A后B→SELECTED_ALL_18或NO_ELIGIBLE_CONDITION。
105: 一个候选符合另一档压力不等于符合自身目标，禁止换标签、clamp或删seed。
106: 
107: 压力p_seed先取每资源[0,W)busy区间精确测度/W的最大值，再对10seed平均。H/L DMR各先逐run
108: 按组计数再对seed平均。light p∈[0,.5)、H/L均值≤.01；medium p∈[.5,.8)；near p∈[.8,1]，
109: 后两H均值∈[.01,.80]且每seed至少一个节点在data-ready之后有正的实际资源busy等待。
110: 不把policy idle wait全部当资源忙。压力三段对有效p互斥穷尽；其他谓词可拒绝全部，保留OFF_TARGET。
111: 
112: 所有10seed的H-only与LOW控制分别无服务重叠且所需组按原deadline完成才有资格。合法控制miss
113: 或无隔离见证是有效负面、配置INELIGIBLE，继续既定trial；控制变换不合法、缺组、非法trace、
114: 缺失/非有限统计、hash/重放不符是INVALID/INCOMPLETE，停止整个计划，不当作零机会。
115: 
116: 天然不可行证书使用**离线私有真实代价**，不能进scheduler或M1R。数学实数下界与实际计算分开：
117: 先取compatible实际duration最小值m、合法资源对通信最小值c。计算版使用与现引擎相同分组的
118: binary64 RN64：根从真实arrival开始；R_v=max(arrival,max_pred RN64(B_pred+c_min))；
119: B_v=RN64(R_v+m_v)。与RN64(arrival+relative_deadline)比较，只有严格大于才CERTIFIED_NATURALLY_INFEASIBLE。
120: 等号不证不可行；B≤deadline不证可行；B2控制miss不证所有调度皆不可行。有限非负RN64单调，
121: 同分组归纳得B_v≤合法执行finish；溢出/NaN直接拒绝，不用任意epsilon或相对时间减法补救。
122: 控制成功只证明改到达控制的见证，不证明MAIN或原H-only流的可调度性。
123: 
124: 选型后的CI是同校准seed、同选中配置下的条件性描述；不创造独立确认数据，也不更换CI算法。

126: ## D07 — M1历史范围与M1R_PATH_V2
127: 
128: 旧`M1_READY_PREFIX_V1`保持原定义/源hash，只是历史诊断。它的阴性不能否定remaining-H-path
129: 稀缺资源机制。本版拟议stage观察器为`M1R_PATH_V2`，精确规范见`P2_diagnostic_spec_v2.json`。
130: 
131: E的在域状态：至少一个dependency-ready未开始节点，且至少一个已到达H服务有未开始节点。
132: 先按原B2顺序选最多4 ready任务；保留原§44的2–4 ready oracle范围：只有1 ready时记覆盖不足，
133: 不把它当零机会或执行单任务oracle。K不必含ready H，目标H可以是正在运行前驱之后的公开后继。
134: 目标最多两个H服务，事先按deadline/service ID选定；各完整公开DAG最多16节点。
135: 
136: 初始日历只固定可见running任务的估计释放，不查看真实finish。若任何busy任务elapsed≥estimate，
137: 不能为已知仍busy的单元编造now释放；记覆盖不足，不加epsilon或看队列。
138: K固定顺序，枚举兼容映射≤6^4。基准用同有理算术的greedy EFT。K间及可见running日历都固定。
139: 随后对选中H服务的全部未放置节点作固定Kahn顺序、deadline/service/node ties和EFT gap插入，
140: 只投影已到达公开图。后继只作为预测，不冒充actual completed，不做suffix组合优化。
141: 两个target+K总计≤36个投影节点，当前running上下文≤6；没有未来到达、真值或反馈到实际B2。
142: 
143: 局部L约束改为K内每个L节点finish≤max(基准该节点finish,该服务absolute deadline)。这是新增
144: 诊断slack规则，不是全局L-P95 1.10预算，也不证明L整个DAG结束不劣化。目标是选中H服务
145: 投影完成时刻均值最小，同分按K资源向量字典序。最优枚举包含基准；b为基准H均值finish-now，
146: o为最优对应值；Δ=b-o≥0ms，g=Δ/b。全部来自公共binary64值的精确有理运算，区别于runtime浮点。
147: 
148: FULL：ready全部落在2..4内、所有pending H服务≤2、DAG/日历/枚举完整、running释放可预测。
149: 截断、单ready或估计过期：COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT；可存有限子空间描述，但不计hit或率，不补零。
150: 真正g=0只表示该完整测试空间无改善；0<g<tau为低于实质cutoff，不与零混称。
151: 缺snapshot/非法/独立复算不符是INCOMPLETE/INVALID证据并闭锁，不是合法覆盖不足。
152: 
153: 非科学§32解释例：K含H前驱2与L可选NPU6/CPU6.5；固定suffix含H的NPU4，故投影完成10对6，
154: 而新局部L cap=max(6,12)=12容纳6.5。只是检查机制定义能覆盖已登记解释，不是科学结果或CI。
155: 
156: M2仍为相同输入B1/B3/B4/B5相对B2的H-DMR、L-P95、资源等待等配对描述；不作为独立因果
157: mapping证据。旧M1不进入v2门禁，不声称M1R或M2验证了候选，也没有提出online候选公式。

166: E或F=0则p未定义，不能写0；部分覆盖从p排除但在E保留，不悄悄消失。120个seed/loaded-condition
167: 单元均须满足相应覆盖，且无缺失无效记录。共同seed-index paired bootstrap保持10,000/
168: 20261018/type7不变；只分析seed p，不按view/node/100服务当独立样本；选中校准CI仍为描述。
169: 
170: 支持：全部12条件CI下端≥f且各有≥8seed p≥f，全部coverage≥4/5。
171: 不支持：全部coverage=1且全部12条件CI上端<f、各≤2seed p≥f。
172: 其余INCONCLUSIVE；missing/invalid/incomplete不能当阴性。没有执行时仍NOT_EVALUATED。
173: 阴性只是不支持此注册有界公共预测观察器下的稳定机会，按协议停止推进；不证明所有策略、

6: ## Lower-bound validity and limits
7: 
8: For a service with compatible set A_v and actual resource-dependent duration d(v,r), define
9: m_v=min_{r∈A_v} d(v,r). For edge u→v define e_uv as the minimum legal transfer delay over compatible
10: source/destination assignments (zero if the same unit is legal; otherwise the minimum nonnegative
11: L+b/B). Define ℓ_v=m_v+max_u(ℓ_u+e_uv), empty predecessor maximum0, and service LB=max_sink ℓ_v.
12: These are offline quantities: no true d(v,r), LB or private assignment alternatives go to the scheduler.
13: 
14: In the exact nonnegative time model this is a valid lower bound: each chosen duration≥m_v and chosen
15: edge delay≥e_uv; any legal start follows each predecessor completion plus its actual transfer. Induction
16: over the DAG gives completion(v)−arrival≥ℓ_v. Independently minimizing adjacent choices may be
17: inconsistent as a real assignment, but makes the bound more optimistic, never less optimistic.
18: Thus LB>D is sufficient for impossibility in that model, while LB≤D is not a feasibility proof.
19: B2 missing an isolated deadline also cannot establish impossibility of every other schedule.
20: 
21: IMPLEMENTATION NOT VERIFIED: v1 gives the recurrence in prose and does not implement it. Its final
22: payload and existing engine use binary64 additions; exact-real induction alone does not certify a
23: particular rounded relative-LB implementation. Before a future implementation, pin conservative
24: arithmetic and absolute-arrival/deadline comparison semantics, including equality, without adding an
25: arbitrary epsilon or changing thresholds. One possible route to review is a monotone absolute-time
26: recurrence using the same operation grouping as the approved engine, but this audit does not choose
27: or implement it. Future tests must include rounding boundaries. This qualification is part of the
28: D06/D10 numerical/metric contract gaps (V2-F07), not proof that a current scientific classification failed.

30: ## Controls and pressure classification
31: 
32: H_ISOLATED changes arrival times and removes L. LOW_COMPETITION changes arrival times of all services.
33: Even with identical private keyed durations/deadlines, they are different workload artifacts with
34: different hashes from MAIN. A successful control is only a witness for its modified admissions, not
35: a proof that the original H-only arrival stream or mixed MAIN stream is schedulable.
36: 
37: The proposed G uses a conservative serial compute/communication sum plus deadline and1ms. A serial
38: topological schedule fits this bound in the exact model; the actual chosen B2 control must still be
39: checked for inter-service nonoverlap and deadlines. G's max-service domain (parent versus retained
40: services), floating conversion and overlap failure classification require the D04/D06 pinning in
41: V2-F03/V2-F07. The original registered §37 permits exclusive or low-competition checks; no extra
42: miss tolerance is supplied by that permission.

55: ## M1 baseline membership and single-H structural limit
56: 
57: Under a fully pinned local append model, the greedy baseline maps every task compatibly, and its
58: L completion equals its own L constraints. It is therefore feasible in the enumeration; optimum≤baseline,
59: so Δ≥0 and b>0 follow from positive H estimates. This is a mathematical design property, NOT execution
60: of the unimplemented observer. Tie order, exact busy/data estimates and denominator contract still need
61: specification/behavioral tests. No actual M1 ratio or project outcome is computed here.
62: 
63: All H tasks precede L in fixed B2 order. If K contains exactly one H, that H is first. Its greedy EFT
64: already minimizes its own finish against the fixed public snapshot; later L assignments cannot alter
65: the first H's predicted finish in the append model. Therefore the constrained H optimum equals that
66: baseline in every such state. This is a structural statement, independent of trial results.

73: realization, scheduler or simulator was instantiated to obtain it.
